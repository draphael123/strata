the vale is dark, and you are paid to change that
Fendmere hired a lamplighter and you are what turned up. There is a fire, still warm, that someone else built; water below; old stone around you raised for a reason nobody left written down. Past the firelight the vale is black to the edges.
Your lamp burns oil, and a beacon costs a good measure of it. Every light you leave standing is a piece of your own safety spent on ground that will still be lit when you come back through it at midnight.
